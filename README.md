# TuDO — Aspirant Execution Companion

> **The ultimate execution companion for students & competitive exam aspirants.**  
> Transform massive exam syllabi into daily micro-targets and execute relentlessly.

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Vision

Preparing for competitive exams like **UPSC, JEE, NEET, GATE, CAT, USMLE, or Bar Exam** requires conquering intimidating, multi-subject syllabi. 

Traditional to-do apps fail because flat lists become overwhelming, while complex project boards are disconnected from daily study routines. **TuDO** bridges this gap by offering a **structured 5-tier Goal Blueprint** synced with a **real-time Daily Execution Engine**.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

- **🎯 Syllabus Blueprint Tree**: Organize your entire exam preparation into structured, non-intimidating tiers (`Goal` &rarr; `Phase` &rarr; `Subject Section` &rarr; `Topic Task` &rarr; `Revision Sub-tasks`).
- **⚡ Step-Slice Multi-Tap Progress**: Break topics into progressive execution steps (e.g. `1. Lecture Watched` &rarr; `2. PYQs Practiced` &rarr; `3. Revision Complete`).
- **🔄 Synced Daily Dispatch**: Drill into any subject module, select pending topics, and dispatch them straight to **Today's Execution**. Progress automatically updates your master syllabus tree in real-time.
- **📅 Interactive Calendar & Timeline**: View target date assignments and jump directly to exact syllabus topics with 1 tap.
- **🛡️ 100% Offline & Private**: Operates entirely on your device with local storage and full JSON Backup/Export. No servers, no accounts, zero tracking.
- **⚡ PWA Ready**: Installable as a native app on Android, iOS, Windows, and Mac.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS, Frosted Glassmorphism, Modern Executive Dark/Light Mode
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **PWA**: Vite PWA & Workbox Offline Caching
